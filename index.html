Beyond the Code.

I'm a B.Tech student in Computer Science and Engineering (Artificial Intelligence) at Babu Banarasi Das University, Lucknow. Machine learning and software development are what I want to build my career on.

I learn by building. RoboDog, Placement Predictor and ARIA are three AI-based projects that each started as a real problem: a robot that navigates on its own, a way to estimate placement chances, and a planner that turns goals into an action plan. Alongside them I've deployed ML apps you can try live.

Outside the editor I debate in Hindi, take part in technical and literary events, and have worked on discipline teams at IPL, concerts and college events, and I head the discipline team of the cultural club AAINA. Those habits make me a steady teammate: calm under pressure, clear in communication, and always learning.
